Step 1 · Make your choices

Plan your campus day

A few quick choices are all your backpack needs.

01 How many classes do you have?

We will add one paper notebook for every two classes.

02 Which weather conditions apply?

Choose every condition that fits your day.

03 Will you exercise outdoors today?

Add a sports drink only when your day includes outdoor activity.

04 Which devices do you need?

Choose either one, both, or leave both at home.

✓

1 class means 1 notebook. No weather extra, sports drink or device selected.
