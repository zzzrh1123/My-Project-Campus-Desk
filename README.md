# Campus Desk

Campus Desk is a four-page website designed for university students.

It brings reading resources, classroom note-taking, daily campus preparation
and active study breaks together in one place.

## Audience

This website is for university students who want a simple way to:

- find reliable learning resources online;
- organise their class notes more easily;
- prepare their backpacks before leaving for class;
- choose suitable physical activities during study breaks.

## Pages

### Home

The homepage introduces the website through a digital university study desk.

### Study Shelf

The Study Shelf combines reliable reading resources with a simple tool for
taking and organising notes during class.

### Campus Loadout

The Campus Loadout recommends useful items based on the student's daily
classes, activities, weather and time away from their dormitory.

### Active Breaks

The Active Breaks page recommends suitable activities based on the user's
available time, location, energy level and equipment.

## Planned Interactions

- Search and filter learning resources by category.
- Write, organise, copy and download class notes.
- Generate a backpack packing list from the user's choices.
- Recommend suitable activities from the user's preferences.
- Display simple stick-figure animations for selected activities.
